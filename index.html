Description

The FS-20C is a compact fingertip pulse oximeter that measures blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂) and pulse rate in about eight seconds. Clip it onto a fingertip and the OLED display shows both readings together with a real-time plethysmograph waveform.

It is intended for spot-checking at home, in clinics, and during sports or high-altitude activity. The display rotates through four viewing directions, and the device switches off automatically when no finger is detected, so a set of batteries lasts through roughly 30 hours of measurement.

Key Features

  • SpO₂ and pulse rate readings in approximately 8 seconds
  • OLED display with plethysmograph waveform and 4-direction rotation
  • One-button operation; automatic shut-off after 8 seconds without a finger
  • Low-battery indicator; runs on two AAA batteries
  • Fits finger widths from 8 mm to 25 mm — suitable for adults and older children
  • Weighs about 50 g without batteries; includes lanyard for portability
Important: This device is intended for spot-check measurement and general wellness monitoring. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. Readings may be affected by nail polish, cold fingers, poor circulation, or strong ambient light. Consult a healthcare professional about any medical concern.
